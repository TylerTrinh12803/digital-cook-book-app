Logos for the downloaded slides (optional)

Put up to two logo images here, as PNG files with a transparent background:

    logo-1.png   shown first, at the bottom left of each slide
    logo-2.png   shown next to it

Use the official logo files from your company's brand resources. Wide logos look best
(about 3 to 5 times wider than tall). Then click "Download slides (.pptx)" again.

Without these files the slides show the company names as text instead.
Logos are only used when the tracker runs from the start file (not when index.html is
opened directly).
